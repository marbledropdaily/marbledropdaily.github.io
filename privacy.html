Marble Drop Daily ("we") makes marble-race videos for the YouTube channel @MarbleDropDaily. This page explains what data we use to run the game and what we do with it.

YouTube API Services

Marble Drop Daily uses YouTube API Services. By taking part you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What we read

We read the public comments on our own videos through the YouTube Data API, to find the comments that ask to join a race ("race") or to leave the queue ("erase"). From each comment we use the commenter's YouTube channel ID, their public display name (handle) and when the comment was posted. We read the comment text only to check whether it is one of those words; we don't store it.

What we store

  • Your YouTube channel ID and display name, and when we first and last saw a comment from you.
  • Your entries (when you asked to race) and the races you took part in, with your finishing place.
  • For each comment, only which video it was on, when it was posted and what it did (entered, left, other).

We don't collect emails, private data from your Google account, cookies or any data outside YouTube. This website has no cookies, analytics or trackers.

How we use it

Only to run the game: to draw the marbles of each race, to show your display name on your marble in the video, and to keep race results and statistics (such as wins). Your display name appears in our public videos because you asked to race. We don't sell, share or use this data for advertising.

Uploading our videos

We use the YouTube Data API with permission from our own channel only, to upload our videos to it. We never ask for access to your account.

How long we keep it

Entries expire if they aren't picked within a few days. Race results are kept for the game's history and statistics. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see below); we will remove it within 30 days, except what is already part of a published video.

Your choices

  • Comment "erase" on the latest video to leave the queue.
  • Email marbledropdaily@gmail.com with your channel name to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted.

Children

Marble Drop Daily is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
