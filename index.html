A marble race every day

Marble Drop Daily is a YouTube Shorts channel: every day a new marble race drops down a different track, and the marbles carry the names of the people who asked to race.

Watch on YouTube

How to join

  1. Watch the latest video on the channel.
  2. Comment just race.
  3. Your marble, with your YouTube name, races in one of the next videos. Comment erase to leave the queue.

When fewer people join than a race needs, grey CPU marbles fill the grid. Races are drawn at random among the people waiting.
