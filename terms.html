Marble Drop Daily is a free game run on the YouTube channel @MarbleDropDaily. By commenting to join a race you agree to these terms and to the YouTube Terms of Service.

How the game works

  • A comment that is just the entry word ("race") on the latest video enters you in the next races; "erase" takes you out of the queue.
  • Each race has a limited number of marbles. The marbles are drawn at random among the people waiting, and entries expire after a while. Being entered doesn't guarantee a place in a particular race.
  • Races are simulated by a computer from a random seed. Results are final.
  • There are no prizes, purchases or payments.

Names

Your marble shows your public YouTube display name. Names with offensive words or that impersonate others may be left out of races, at our discretion.

Changes and availability

We may change the rules, pause or stop the game at any time. The videos and the game are provided as they are, without warranties.
